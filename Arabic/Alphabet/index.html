The Arabic Alphabet الحروف الهجائية

Arabic is written from right to left in a cursive script. Almost every letter connects to its neighbors, changing its shape based on whether it appears in isolation, at the beginning, middle, or end of a word.

💡
Key Rule for English Speakers: Arabic letters have up to 4 shapes (Isolated, Initial, Medial, Final). Six special letters (أ, د, ذ, ر, ز, و) are non-connectors: they never connect to the letter after them, leaving a tiny visual gap.
