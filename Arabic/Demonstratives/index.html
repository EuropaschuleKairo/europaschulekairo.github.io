📚 Egyptian Demonstrative Rules (p. 15)

Egyptian Arabic uses three simple words for "this is / that is / these are":

دا da Masculine

This is / That is for singular male words & masculine nouns.

دا راجل عجوز. da ragel Agooz.
دي di / dee Feminine

This is / That is for singular female words & words ending with ة (teh marboota).

دي بنت جميلة. di bent gameela.
دول duul / dool Plural People

These are / Those are specifically for groups of human beings.

دول ناس كتير. dool nas kateer.
